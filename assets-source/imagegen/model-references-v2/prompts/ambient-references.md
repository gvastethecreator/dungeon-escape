# Ambient reference generation record

## Shared contract

Each object used a separate built-in `imagegen` call. The chest supplied the locked low-poly
finish, pixel-PBR restraint, neutral light, background, and horizontal three-view layout. Every
sheet keeps one exact arrangement across front, right profile, and rear-left three-quarter. The
spike plate uses its decorated top as the face-on front and exposes its underside in the third
view. Prompts banned scene floors, text, frames, particles, extra pieces, unsupported parts,
perspective drift, cropping, and view changes.
